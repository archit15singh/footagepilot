# Resolve reference ideas and implementation order

Research date: 12 November 2026. These priorities are engineering judgments, not output-quality benchmark scores. Search was performed in signed-in Instagram for `davinci resolve`, then the visible `davinci_resolve_tips` account was inspected. No likes, saves, comments, follows or posts were performed.

| Priority | Idea | Expected benefit | Effort | Evidence and limitation | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Explicit cut/beat map plus frame rounding | Avoid drifting cuts; inspect rhythm without pretending to hear | Low | Known authored score grid is trustworthy timing; detected audio onsets remain candidates | Implemented in Resolve job preparation/native marker script; native markers/export verified |
| 2 | Individual native timeline clips and retained project | Human finishing and revisions remain possible | Medium | Installed vendor scripting docs; internal Lua version/project queries passed | Native assembly/export and local DRP verified |
| 3 | Selective colour masks and layered windows | Recreate the saved collage style with controlled reveals | Medium/high | [Masking example, credited to kortafilms](https://www.instagram.com/davinci_resolve_tips/reel/DRBdgfpEZtJ/). Playback advanced from a coastline polygon outline to an elliptical mask over a park shot; the downloaded frame sequence also shows selective sky/scene appearance changes. This establishes visible mask geometry and local grading, not a mask-transition implementation or an audible transition | Research candidate; not implemented in Resolve v1 |
| 4 | Reusable native effects rather than plugin presets | Faster repeated styling with fewer external dependencies | Medium | [Effects trick, credited to sheikh.dng](https://www.instagram.com/davinci_resolve_tips/reel/DYlwMQ6I40_/), discovered from account grid/caption only; not yet watched or reproduced | Unverified lead; no implementation claim |
| 5 | Shot-aware colour matching | More coherent footage while preserving skin/sky detail | High | Official colour training; one creator's exposure/temperature values cannot be applied to every source | Defer until native timeline/export works |
| 6 | Camera-motion match/spin transitions | More convincing transitions than effects pasted over unrelated shots | High | Previously inspected saved spin reference needs similar subject motion and matching capture; still photos cannot reproduce real camera action | Requires suitable moving footage; do not fake equivalence |

The native import/export loop now works. The next largest gaps are native title/collage/transition fidelity and planner integration, followed by controlled visual iterations against saved references. Do not claim Resolve outranks Edits from one cut-only export. Negative visual feedback from the earlier photo slideshow remains valid: technical decode and on-grid cuts do not establish that a film is attractive.
