# Two personalities finding balance

A separate FootagePilot reel about Archit and Divya. Build from existing Google Photos footage. Aim for 12–18 seconds, vertical.

## Story and cut

1. **Show two rhythms.** Open on one expressive gesture or reaction and answer it with a quieter take from the other person. Pick footage where the difference is visible. Avoid assigning either person a personality label.
2. **Find the connection.** Match a glance, gesture, or movement across the two shots. Briefly show them side by side if it makes the connection easier to see. Let the quicker side slow down until the shots feel in sync.
3. **End together.** Resolve to one candid shared moment or an image with both people clearly visible. Leave it on screen long enough to register. Use the strongest source sound, such as a laugh or room ambience, to carry the join. Keep any opening text short.

## Source selection

Search Google Photos for Divya and look for nearby clips of Archit from the same outing. Prefer a matched pair and a shared moment from one scene. If the archive has no matching pair, build around a visible gesture and a clear two-shot. Keep downloads, source links, and the working edit in ignored local project storage.

Before sharing a cut, verify that the two opening actions read without explanation, both people are visible at the ending, and the entire export decodes.
